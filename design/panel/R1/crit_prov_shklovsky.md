# Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky), R1

**Anchor used:** Shklovsky, "Art as Device". Habit devours perception. Art's purpose is to make the stone stony by prolonging perception and making the form difficult. A device that has become automatic no longer works as art.

**Observed:**
- Landing (dr1__1280.png): block widths on one scale make the 8x80 figure look unlike any chart I know. The white thread jumps from Languages down to Security and back up. The R0 hero (dm0_1280.png) was a stock "Docker connections" card with a lime CTA and a stat row.
- Map (dr1_map_sel_1280.png): a glowing force-directed nebula, with white spokes from a selected node and a neighbour list on the right.
- Concept page (dr1_concepts_c_1280.png): a tinted banner, a three-number stat row, tag chips, and a long list of "-> AMPL / AWK / csh" rows.
- Chrome on every page: "~/developermap", "$ 80 KNOWLEDGE GRAPHS", "/ search".

**Findings:**
1. [premise] The landing figure is the one place the site roughens perception. The eye has to follow the thread through unequal blocks, and the eye cannot read the width differences at a glance. The ~100-word explanatory paragraph beside it then explains the figure. That is acceptable, but it spends part of the strangeness.
2. [defect] The map is the automatized form of "network visualisation": the force-directed hairball with a hub and spokes. Nothing in dr1_map_sel_1280.png makes the viewer see Docker's links anew. Selecting a node gives a neighbour list, a standard readout. The landing figure's device (shared scale and the thread) is not carried into the map.
3. [claim] The terminal costume (cursor block, ~/, $, xterm colours) is a recognised genre, so it reads as familiar rather than strange. It risks being the new automatism that replaces R0's lime-on-black "dev tool" look.
4. [defect] The concept page (dr1_concepts_c_1280.png) is a conventional reference layout. "C - 17 graphs - 6 regions" is shown as three bare numbers, with none of the landing thread. The most shared concept loses its signature form on its own page.
5. [claim] I am not objecting to the plain 4,656-row link list. A reference listing is not meant to be art.

**Verdict:** OBJECT

**Requested change:**
- Give the map the landing's device. For example, lay the selected concept's links out as the white thread across the 8 region rows, or draw edges as the landing's thread so selecting a concept visibly re-routes it.
- On concept pages, replace the three-number stat row with a small thread figure for that concept, drawn the same way as the landing's C thread.
