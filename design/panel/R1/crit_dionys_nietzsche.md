# Critic: Dionysian Excess (Nietzsche) - R1

**Anchor used:** The Birth of Tragedy: Apollonian measure (the principium individuationis, the dream-image, the bounded form) married to Dionysian force (the rupture of boundaries, the swarm). Either alone is a failure: Apollo without Dionysus is a corpse, Dionysus without Apollo is barbarism.

**Observed:**
- Landing (dr1__1280.png): 8 rows x 80 blocks at one scale (Apollo), pierced by a white thread snaking through 17 graphs and 6 regions, dots on every crossing (Dionysus). The h1 states the rupture: "1,397 of them turn up in more than one graph."
- Map selected (dr1_map_sel_1280.png): a dim, petal-shaped swarm of 4,656 points; Docker's links flare white and the neighbour list is ordered.
- Concept C (dr1_concepts_c_1280.png): quiet light man-page dossier; the 17-graph frenzy is a row of chips and a number.
- R0 (dm0_1280.png): a lime-on-black terminal panel, three tidy examples; no force.

**Findings:**
1. [premise] The landing is the marriage: the thread breaks the grid and the grid gives it a stage. Better than R0, where measure had no counterweight. Not a defect.
2. [claim] The map at rest is Dionysian in mass but timid in intensity: the swarm is dim and the fury arrives only on selection. Restraint here is deliberate (disclosure says nearness is "linked", not measured), so this is a choice, not a defect.
3. [claim] The concept page returns to pure Apollo: legible, but C's cross-graph nature is not felt there. The change of register (dark terminal to light man-page) reads as intentional dream/waking, so I do not object.
4. [defect, minor] On the landing the thread runs through block labels ("Go", "C", "VMs" nearly lost), so force obscures measure where it should reveal it.

**Verdict:** PASS. What remains is minor and not a defect of the whole.

**Requested change:** (optional) route or mask the thread so it does not overprint block labels; let the thread's white also touch the concept page header.
